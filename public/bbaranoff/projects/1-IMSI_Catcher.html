Hacking 2G (Fooling MS : Mobile Station, the 2G phone)

Easy ! The MS doesn’t ask authentication from BTS (Base Transceiver Station, the relay antenna). So what to do to intercept ? Be a BTS… and that’s all just spoof the public values of the BTS (mcc,mnc exemple 208,15 for FreeMobile 208,01 for Orange, etc) and broadcast a stronger signal and it is done. How the implement a 2G BTS ? there are open-sourced projects like
